The shared reliability pattern

Five of the Workers above (funraise-sync, quickbooks-sync, benevity-import, donation-rollups, mailchimp-sync) share one shape, added after an outside reviewer found that a bare fetch() to Monday's API has no retry of its own — a rate limit or a transient 5xx just silently drops the write.
