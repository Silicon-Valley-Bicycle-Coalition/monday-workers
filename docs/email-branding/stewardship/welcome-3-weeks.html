Thank you again for being part of the Silicon Valley Bicycle Coalition community! For over 30 years, SVBC has championed bike-friendly communities and safer streets.

Your generosity helps make our work possible, from advocating for safer streets and better biking infrastructure to expanding bicycling education and creating more opportunities for people to ride. Behind every initiative, community partnership, and advocacy win is a dedicated team working to make Silicon Valley a safer, healthier, and more connected place.

We’d love to introduce you to the teams whose work you’re helping advance. Each brings unique expertise and passion to our shared mission.

Meet the SVBC Teams Behind the Movement

Programs Team: Helps more people experience the joy and freedom of bicycling. From teaching bike safety and pedestrian skills to helping adults get started with bike commuting and organizing community rides, this team makes bicycling more accessible for people of all ages and abilities.

Advocacy Team: Works with communities, cities, and regional partners to make multimodal options, connectivity, and affordability priorities. SVBC advocates from the local to the state level to advance policies and programs that prioritize people-centered planning processes and design.

Development & Engagement Team: Builds the relationships and resources that sustain the bike movement. From fundraising and donor stewardship to signature events and volunteer engagement, this team connects people who believe in our mission with opportunities to make a difference.

Your generosity helps bring all of this work together. Every team plays an important role in advancing our mission, and your investment helps ensure they have the resources to keep moving this work forward.

Thank you for being part of what makes this work possible. We’re grateful to have you alongside us!
